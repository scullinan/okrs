# Team OKR Examples

Use these examples to guide concise coaching. Do not paste large examples unless the user asks.

## ACCID Rewrite Pattern

Weak Objective:

> Improve product quality.

Stronger Objective:

> Reduce the defect rate by 50% by the end of the quarter.

Why it is stronger:

- Clear: defines what product quality means.
- Challenging: sets a meaningful improvement target.
- Deadline-oriented: includes the timeframe.

## Alignment Example

Company focus:

> Expand the customer base in Europe.

Misaligned team Objective:

> Redesign the website to improve conversion rates for U.S. visitors by the end of Q2.

Aligned team Objective:

> Optimize the website experience for German, French, and Spanish users, increasing conversions by 20% by the end of Q2.

Why it is stronger:

- It supports the geographic expansion priority.
- It directs the team's work toward the relevant market.

## Zoom Example

Too zoomed out:

> Become the market leader in digital payments.

Too zoomed in:

> Reduce checkout screen load time by 0.5 seconds.

Balanced focus:

> Optimize the mobile checkout experience to increase conversion rates by 15% by the end of Q3.

The load-time improvement may be a strong KR, but by itself it is too task-like for a team Objective.

## Output To Outcome KR Example

Objective:

> Be recognized as a great place to work by the end of the year, increasing our internal satisfaction survey score by 2 points within six months.

Output KR:

> Promote at least two public lectures per month with reference professionals, with a minimum of 10 lectures by December.

Why test:

> Why is this KR important for the Objective?

Possible answer:

> Because these lectures could increase employee engagement, which should improve satisfaction survey results.

Outcome KR:

> At least 10% of internal satisfaction survey responses mention the open talks as a reason for high ratings.

The Output KR tracks activity. The Outcome KR checks whether the activity contributed to the Objective.

## Complementary KR Example

Objective:

> Increase Lean Inception Training sales by 30% by year-end.

Output KR:

> Create a new landing page for the Lean Inception training by the end of the month.

Outcome KR:

> Reduce Customer Acquisition Cost for new students from $6 to $3 over the next three months.

Complementary KR:

> Maintain the monthly marketing budget for Lean Inception at $2,000.

Why the Complementary KR matters:

- Reducing CAC only helps sales if reach is not reduced by a budget cut.
- The Complementary KR protects a condition needed for the Outcome KR to translate into sales growth.

## Binary KR Example

Objective:

> Improve checkout reliability during peak traffic by the end of Q3.

Output KR:

> Launch the new checkout service by September 30.

Outcome KR:

> Maintain 99.95% checkout availability during peak traffic for four consecutive weeks after launch.

Complementary KR:

> Keep checkout error rate below 0.5% during migration.

The launch is binary and therefore an Output KR. The Outcome and Complementary KRs make the success condition meaningful.
